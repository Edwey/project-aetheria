3. NON-NEGOTIABLE GAME RULES (carry these from the prototype)
1. Three forms: Slime (liquid), Wisp (gas), Stone (solid). EVERY form has EVERY verb:
   move, jump or hop, climb, glide or float, squeeze, hover, gather, craft, build,
   swim, emote. Only the way of doing it and the perks differ.
2. Advantages only, never disadvantages. No terrain, weather, wind or water may slow,
   slip or weigh down any form. If a change makes any form worse at any verb, revert it.
3. Every creature has a Core that keeps it together and is where items fly in.
   Slime: shape-match centre. Wisp: tether centre (motes never farther than 2.2 m).
   Stone: rigid centre. The Core and inventory survive form switches.
4. Wind and currents are STEADY by default: they never move, slow or lift a creature.
   Hold Ride (Shift) and they carry and boost you (tailwind up to +40% speed, headwinds
   never slow you, updrafts lift you). Same rule for river currents.
5. Peaceful: no combat, no damage, no drowning, no hunger.
6. Pure gameplay modules: src/gameplay/* import nothing (no THREE, no DOM) and run in Node.
7. Budget $0: free tiers and CC0 or attributed-CC-BY assets only.

9. WORLD AND WATER (new work, stages R1 to R4)
World API (R1): WorldSet = list of terrain bodies. Each body has its own height grid
(0.5 m cells), bounds, colliders and flora. ground(x,z) queries the body that contains
the point; outside any body is VOID (creatures fall into the cloud sea and respawn at
the last safe ground, with no damage). Landmass 240 x 240 m, rendered as 40 m chunk
meshes (6 x 6) with per-chunk instanced flora; the isometric orthographic camera only
needs the chunks in view. Colliders stay in the global 4 m broadphase grid.
Sky islands (R2): 3 islands, about 60 m wide, centres 30 to 50 m above sea level, one
resource or landmark each. Reached by thermals above dune crests, mushroom trampolines,
glide and float, and bridges. Test that every island is reachable by every form.

Water rules (ALL forms, rule 2 applies): no drowning, no damage, no speed loss, no
breath cost for being in water. Water has a surface level per body and a bed (height grid).
Per form perks:
- Slime: floats and swims at the surface, dives when you hold F, and has a skip-dash
  (charged jump released at speed bounces off the surface up to 3 times).
- Wisp: skimming over water counts as "low" (breath refills, free), and it can dive
  in as mist at normal speed.
- Stone: sinks to the bed and WALKS it at full ground speed, never needs to surface,
  gathers seabed resources fastest (one hit), ignores currents.
Inputs in water: Space = swim up or jump out, F = dive while submerged (pound is
replaced while submerged). Treat these as a starting proposal and tune in playtests.
Currents: rivers have a flow field. It only affects you while Riding (rule 4).
Types and content:
- Sea (R3): surrounds the landmass, beach band, depth up to about 6 m near shore.
  Resources: pearl, kelp fibre (counts as fibre).
- Lakes and rivers (R4): inside plains, grove and taiga. Lakebed clay. Rivers follow splines.
- Swamp (R4): shallow (1 m or less), murky tint, reeds (fibre), glowing spores. Wading at full speed.
- Frozen water (R4): ice sheets you can walk on with NEUTRAL physics (no slipping). Ground
  pound cracks a ring open and drops ice crystals.
Building on water (R4): shallow water takes normal foundations with stilts to the bed;
deep water needs a "raft" floor piece that floats and snaps to other rafts (Raft reference).
Cheap rendering: one transparent plane per body of water, vertex-shader waves,
colour from a precomputed depth stored in vertex colours (no depth texture, no
reflection, no refraction), shoreline foam from the depth band, underwater tint from fog
plus a CSS overlay when the camera target is submerged. Fake caustics only in Medium+.
Buoyancy for soft bodies: per-particle upward acceleration proportional to depth below
the surface (clamped), water drag, tuned so rule 2 holds. Write the numbers in WATER.md.
Water tests: no form is slower in water than on land at the same input (within 10%);
the slime surfaces; the stone walks the bed and gathers; the wisp refills breath
over water; currents act only while Riding; no NaN anywhere.

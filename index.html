WASD or arrows move. Slime and stone: hold Space to charge a jump, release to jump; hold it in the air to glide (stone: crystal sail) and hang briefly at the top. Wisp: tap Space to hop, hold Space to rise (costs breath fast), press V to float and hold your height (costs breath slowly); otherwise it sinks. Breath refills on the ground. Push into a wall to climb (slime sticky, stone pitons, wisp wall-hug). M: melt (slime), pebble (stone), diffuse (wisp). C: spring or condense. F: pound or down-gust. H: heart. Hold Shift to Ride the wind (it never moves you otherwise). Tab switches form. 1 to 8 are emotes. B shows wind. P toggles build mode, [ ] pick a piece, X rotates, Enter places, Del removes. Q/E rotate the camera, wheel zooms, R respawns, T slow-mo.

How to play

Move and look

  • WASD or arrows to move. On a phone use the stick.
  • Q E turn the camera, mouse wheel zooms.
  • Tab changes form. 1 to 8 are emotes. Esc is this menu.

Slime (liquid)

  • Hold Space to squash down, release to jump. The longer you hold, the higher you go.
  • Hold Space in the air to glide.
  • Push into a wall to climb it (needs Stickiness).
  • M melts into a puddle to slip under gaps. F pounds the ground.

Wisp (gas)

  • Tap Space to hop. Hold it to rise, which uses breath fast.
  • Press V to float: you hold your height and breath drains slowly. Otherwise you sink.
  • On the ground your breath refills. Out of breath means no more rising until you touch down.
  • F dives for a shockwave. C condenses, M spreads out.

Stone (solid)

  • Rolls fast and keeps its shape.
  • Push into a wall to climb with pitons. You can hang there as long as you like.
  • Hold Space in the air for the crystal sail. It lands with a shockwave.
  • M shrinks to a pebble to fit through small gaps.

Wind

  • Wind never pushes you around by default, so you can stand still safely.
  • Hold Shift to ride it: tailwinds speed you up, headwinds never slow you, and updrafts lift you.
  • Press B to see the wind arrows.

Gather, craft, build

  • Run into trunks to shake loose wood and sap. Pound geodes and clay. Roll over flowers.
  • Items fly into your Core when you are near.
  • Open Craft and build in this menu. Press P to place things: Enter places, X turns, Del removes.

Physics in plain words

Your slime is a soft jelly made of hundreds of tiny points. It squashes when it lands, stretches when it jumps and wobbles when it stops. The wisp is a cloud of motes held together by its glowing Core. The stone is the same body made completely stiff.

Everyone can do everything. The forms only differ in how they do it and what they are best at.

Welcome
